# SwissTrust Dossier – Updated Strategy & Market Analysis (v2)
Prepared: March 2026
Confidential

## 1. Executive Summary
SwissTrust is a verified tenant identity platform addressing:
- Housing shortage
- Broken application processes
- Identity theft risks

Core innovation: privacy-preserving QR certificates separating verification from data sharing.

## 2. Hybrid Certificate Model
Three modes:
- Directed: pre-authorised recipient
- Open: any verified professional
- On-Request: tenant approval required

## 3. User Types
- Tenants: build profile, generate certificates
- Régies: agencies with tiered access
- Private owners: simplified landlord accounts

## 4. Market Context
- Vacancy rates at historic lows (~1%)
- 50–100 applicants per listing
- High identity theft risk

## 5. Pain / Gain
Tenants: control data, reduce repetition  
Régies: verified dossiers, reduced fraud  
Owners: simple verification tools

## 6. Competitive Positioning
Unique combination:
- Verified identity
- AI verification
- QR certificates
- Works off-platform

## 7. Business Model
- Tenants: free core, premium upgrades
- Régies: SaaS tiers
- Owners: low-cost plans

## 8. Go-To-Market
- Start with tenants
- Expand regionally
- Convert régies via usage

## 9. Acquisition Strategy
- Passive inbound
- Free viewer conversion
- Pilot partnerships
- Enterprise sales

## 10. Financials
Year 4 target:
- 200K tenants
- CHF 7.86M ARR

## 11. Risks
- Adoption resistance
- Competition
- Data security

## 12. The Ask
